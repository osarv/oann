# dyn

The dynamical-system core (the model is docs/settling.md): a `System<T>` is a
tree of modules holding populations of neurons with named state, connections between them as objects, signals and
rules on clocks. It is recorded through handles, laid out once by `Plan` and then settled with nothing allocated.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `system.olang` | `System<T>` (T the state's and the weights' element: F32 to run, F64 for checks), the handles `Pop`, `Conn`, `Mod`, `Sig`, `Clock`, `Rule`; recording (`Module`, `Population`, `Connect`, `Signal`, `Attach`), `Plan`, data in (`Clamp`, `Target`, `TargetValues`, `Set`), `Settle`, `Nudged`, `Reset`, `Warm`, state and parameters out (`State<C>`, `Activity`, `Predict`, `Loss`, `Weights`, `Bias`, `Params`, `ParamGrads`, `Read`), structure while running (`Prune`, `Retire`, `Compact`, `Hold`, `Sparsify`, `Macs`: a settle's multiply-adds as the structure stands) how settles sweep (`Use` a `Sweeping`: Jacobi or Gauss-Seidel, Anderson acceleration's depth - the forward settles' and an exact implicit gradient's adjoint's) and stability (`Gain`, `Contraction`: the spectral bound by power iteration, `Bound`: the norm the certificate holds, `Norm.Spectral` (the default; a settle then stops on each row's Euclidean residual) or `Norm.Sup` (the board's), `Kappa`: the bound in it, warm-started, `Certify`, `Restrain`, `Rate` and `NudgedKappa` (a nudged phase's, the force's coupling counted), `NudgedRate` (the nudged pair's from the free state, the force bounded over the ball the settle can reach), `LessonTolerance`, `BudgetFor`, `BudgetAt`) |
| `neuron.olang` | the cells (`RateCell`, `LifCell`, `LifCell.Kept` for state kept across settles), `Rho`, `Neuron` (rate, spiking, `Custom` through the `Dynamics` trait), `Role`, `Cost`, and the per-element work of a sweep: the residual and step, a spiking time step and window, a readout's force and cost |
| `conn.olang` | `Wiring` (Dense, Symmetric, Csr, Tiles, Events, LowRank) and `Link<T>`: its weights as a view of the parameter region, carrying activities forward and back, its share of the units' incoming mass, pruning (`Score`, `Schedule`, `Prune`, `Revive`, `Hold`, `MaskGrads`, `Live`, `Full`; a tiled connection masked a tile at a time, a low-rank one a rank at a time: `Group`, `Groups`, `Unkeep`), compaction in place (a low-rank one's rank made smaller), and `Sparsify` (a masked dense block stored as sparse rows over its own region) |
| `settle.olang` | `Phase`, `Sweeping`, `Budget`, `Settlement` and the `Engine`: population state double-buffered as `Columns` of cells, every phase side by side on one axis, Jacobi sweeps cut into units (a population's rows of a phase, 128 at most) run on the system's tasks - the same bit for bit for any thread count; Gauss-Seidel sweeps (the populations in a topological order of the one-way connections, each reading what the sweep already wrote); Anderson acceleration (type II, a least-squares combination of the last sweeps, dropped where the residual grows; `Accelerate` gives a learner's adjoint the same, dropped only where its residual grows tenfold); `Spectral`, the coupling's largest singular value (power iteration warm-started from its last vector) |
| `learn.olang` | `AdamW<T>` (`For(s)`: nn/optim's AdamW over a system's region, on the backend it was planned on); `Learner<T>`: a planned system's learning - the rules attached to its modules run on their clock (`Tick`): EP lessons (`Lesson`, `Contrast`: free and nudged phases, the contrast for every wiring into `ParamGrads`), three-factor rules (an eligibility trace per rule times a signal; the local term EP's contrast of the last nudge, or Hebbian), homeostasis (thresholds or biases toward a target rate), delta writes, pruning (`Prune`: weights - tiles, ranks - masked toward a fraction kept on a schedule (linear, or cubic: slowing near the target, the default) by a score taken over its connection's mean and ranked across the module, regrowth where units coincide, loose hidden units retired), custom rules; and gradients at a fixed point (`ImplicitGrad`: exact adjoint, JFB, EP through the drive; `TruncatedGrad`: k adjoint steps from JFB) |
| `bridge.olang` | `Bridge<T>`: a settle as one operation of the autodiff graph (`ad/graph`'s `Custom`) - its input a population's drive, its output another's activities at the free fixed point, an optional second input the starting potentials of every relaxing population (an amortizer's guess); its backward the learner's `ImplicitGrad` by the bridge's `Implicit` (exact, JFB, EP), the input's gradient to the graph and the parameters' into the system's `ParamGrads`; `Settled` (the fixed point as an operation with a zero gradient: the amortizer's target), `Detach`, and `Apply`, which records them; counts of settles, sweeps and adjoint sweeps (M14) |
| `energy.olang` | `Energy<E>`: an energy written as a struct of terms whose value, state gradient and parameter gradient are assembled while compiling by walking E's fields; the terms (`Potential`, `Bilinear`, `Linear`, `Squared`, `LogSumExp`), made from a system's populations and connections; `StateOf` (a row's activities) and `Of` (a system's energy) |
| `store.olang` | `Store<T>`: the top-k associative store over `kernels/sparse` (a seeded random projection, the K strongest cells as the code, reads by `GatherRowsWeighted`, writes by the delta rule through `ScatterRank1`; `Fade` and `Clear` evict, `Familiarity` tells what writes taught) and `Memory<T>`, the store as a connection of a system from a key population to a value population (`Recall` into the value's drive, `Write(gate)`; the rule `Remember` writes on a clock) |
| `mirror.olang` | a planned system on the GPU (`System.Plan(r, on = device.Backend.Gpu)`, or the default backend), built only with `-define Gpu=true`: every region on the device at the host's offsets, the settle (a cuBLAS product a carry over all rows, `kernels/gpu/src/settle.cu`'s fused step with the constant and the nudge, sweeps enqueued ahead and stopped on the device by a converged word, the words read back every `Check` sweeps), EP's contrast and AdamW's step there; what reads or writes host state brings it down or marks it stale; refuses at Plan what has no GPU form |
| `gpu_test.olang` | dyn on the GPU against dyn on the CPU, built only with `-define Gpu=true` (`make gputest`): the fused rate step (`kernels/gpu/kern`'s `Rate`) against `neuron.RateStep` with retired units on every `Rho`, and the residual word against the CPU's sup norm; `GpuRho` maps a `Rho` to the kernel's; a system planned on the GPU against the CPU (the 784-128-10 lessons: the same sweeps, the fixed points within the tolerance; every wiring the GPU runs, retired units, a Euclidean budget; EP's contrast; AdamW bit for bit; the refusals; nothing allocated over 300 lessons) |
| `holo.olang` | `Holo.Grad`: holomorphic EP (Laborieux and Zenke 2022) - the gradient at the free fixed point from N nudged phases settled in complex arithmetic around a circle of radius r (N = 2 is centered EP; the bias falls as r^N), into `ParamGrads` as `Learner.ImplicitGrad` writes them; rate units, dense and symmetric connections, a measurement tool (allocates) |
| `recur.olang` | `Recurrence<T>`: a gated linear recurrence on ad/graph (l = sigmoid(g + G u), z = tanh(B u + b), h = l h_prev + (1 - l) z, y = C h + c), Window steps unrolled over Batch rows and trained by backpropagation through time: `Load` and `Target` a row, `Run` (answers in `Answers`, `Row`), `Learn` (the gradients into `ParamGrads`), `Loss`, `Params`; `Reference`, the recurrence written by hand (the tests' oracle) |
| `sleep.olang` | `Sleeper<T>`: sleep consolidation - `Day` (the store writes the residual of the slow answer, the key rows enter a reservoir of cues), `Night` (cues dreamed with the store's read, the slow weights taught the dreams by `Learner.Tick(Clock.Sleep)` - EP or three-factor rules attached to the Sleep clock - then the store rewritten at dawn at rate 1), `Dream` (a rule that runs the night on a clock), `Parts` (the slow answer, the store's read and its familiarity apart - an agent's recall), and a checkpoint's state (`Generator`/`SetGenerator`, `CueInputs`, `Keep`); its slow part the system's settle or a `recur.Recurrence` (the last argument: the system then supplies the key and value populations and the store, the recurrence answers and is taught the dreams by backpropagation through time); day-time learning by `DayRate` (0, off, by default): before the store's write the slow part takes one step of gradient descent toward the targets less the store's read, by a backtracking (Armijo) line search from DayRate halved at most `Halvings` times - the gradient a recurrence's BPTT, or a system's centered EP lesson nudged by `DayBeta`; `DayLoss`, `DaySteps`, `Declined` |
| `halt.olang` | `Stable`: halting on a stable answer - a row of any step-by-step loop (a settle, a descent, a recurrence) holds once its answer, one or more integers, has been unchanged for k steps (`See`, or `Mark` each part then `Step`; `Holds`, `Run`, `Answer`, `Clear`), allocation-free; `ArgMax`. A settle uses it through `Budget.Stable`: a row (with per-row stops) or the whole settle stops once every readout group's class has held for k sweeps, `Settlement.Halted` |

Programs import the modules (`import "dyn/system"` and so on) and write `system.System<F32>(batch, threads)`,
`neuron.Neuron.Rate(neuron.Rho.Tanh)`, `conn.Wiring.Symmetric`, `settle.Budget()`; after `Plan` (and after attaching
the rules) `learn.Learner<F32>(s)!` teaches it: `l.Lesson(s.Root(), beta)!`, an optimizer's step over `s.E.Params` and
`s.E.Grads`, `l.Tick(system.Clock.Step)!`.

Tests: `olang test dyn/FILE.olang` (`make test` runs them). Sleep replay on dyn: `make sleepreplay` (examples/sleep_replay.olang). MNIST taught by EP on dyn:
`make mnistlearn ARGS="EPOCHS"` (examples/mnist_learn.olang; `make data` first). A transformer with a settle block
through the bridge is `models/hybrid.olang`; `make hybridbench ARGS="STEPS SEED UNITS exact|jfb|ep TOL KAPPA"` trains it
on tiny Shakespeare cold and amortized and tables the sweeps a settle takes in each (bench/hybrid.olang).
